## PlayableX Iterate

**AI-powered playable ad platform for mobile game studios and UA teams** · Business Analyst → Product Manager · 2024–2026

**Problem.** Playable ads are small interactive versions of a mobile game. Studios need many variants to find the one that performs, but each variant was built by hand, so testing was slow and expensive.

**What I did**
- Worked on the product as a business analyst from concept through pilot. After the pilot, PlayableX brought me in-house to lead it as it scaled, and we also built Multiply, a sister product for AI video creatives.
- Shipped AI-driven variant generation with a human-in-the-loop model: AI produces the variants, while designers and QA keep the final call at key decision points.
- Took part in ~100 playable ads from client brief and ideation to delivery, deconstructing each game to pick the core mechanic and hook.
- Used funnel analysis (load → first interaction → end card → CTA) to find where players dropped off, and A/B test results to decide which variants to iterate.
- Tracked IPM, CTR, CPA, engagement, and end-card rates by game, country, and platform to prioritize the backlog.
- Ran client demos and onboarding, and worked with marketing on release announcements.

**Outcome.** Creative production time dropped by close to 50%, and the platform onboarded 15 clients.

## PlayableX Multiply

**AI-powered video creative platform for mobile game studios** · Product Manager · 2025–2026

**Problem.** Studios running user acquisition need a steady flow of fresh video ads across many ad networks, and producing each one by hand caps how many they can test.

**What I did**
- Built Multiply after joining PlayableX in-house, applying the same human-in-the-loop model proven on Iterate: AI generates the videos, and people keep the creative and quality decisions.
- Owned the roadmap and backlog alongside Iterate, prioritizing from client feedback, product metrics, and market analysis.

**Outcome.** AI-supported video creation scaled output well beyond what the team could produce manually, across major ad networks.

## Engaraj

**Vehicle damage claims management platform** · Freelance Product Manager · 2026–present

**Problem.** After an accident, a claim for diminished value, material damage, or loss of use has to move between the insurer, the repair shop, and the vehicle owner. Without a shared view, every party chases the others for status updates.

**What I did**
- Designed stage-by-stage tracking of each damage file, from opening to closure.
- Set up automated email and SMS updates at every stage for the relevant person: the service advisor, the customer, or Engaraj staff.
- Designed fleet dashboards where fleet managers follow the damage processes of all their vehicles, and the related metrics, from a single screen.

**Outcome.** One shared source of truth for every claim, instead of manual follow-up calls between the parties.

## VulnTracker

**Cybersecurity vulnerability triage platform with live, enriched CVE data and AI-driven mitigation** · Freelance Product, UI/UX & R&D · 2026–present

**Problem.** Security teams face a constant stream of new vulnerabilities (CVEs) and have to decide quickly which ones matter to them and how to fix them.

**What I did**
- Supported product and UI/UX design ahead of the pilot.
- Researched new features and prioritized them against the development roadmap and budget.
- Benchmarked competitors to define market positioning, target audience, and goals.

**Outcome.** A clear position going into the pilot: AI-driven mitigation guidance, which none of the competitors we reviewed offered.

## T100 Loyalty

**Loyalty system for a Georgian fuel retailer** · Business Analyst at Fill-Labs · 2025

**Problem.** Customers' loyalty points lived on a physical card, so neither customers nor the company could easily see or manage them.

**What I did**
- Defined user flows and data models for a mobile app and web panels that brought the physical loyalty card into a digital experience.
- Turned business needs into requirements and documentation for design and engineering, managed delivery through GitHub issues, and coordinated UAT.

**Outcome.** Customers can track their loyalty card digitally on mobile, and the company manages the program through web panels.
